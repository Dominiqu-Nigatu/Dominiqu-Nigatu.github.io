About

I enjoy turning ideas into things people can use. I like figuring out how something should work, trying different approaches, and seeing it come together.

I care about the small details: a clear layout, an interaction that feels natural, and code that's easy to come back to. I want the things I build to feel simple and useful.

I'm curious by nature and enjoy learning as I go. Whether I'm working through a problem on my own or sharing ideas with others, there's usually something new to discover.
